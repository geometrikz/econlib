\chapter*{Introduction}
This blueprint is the human-readable companion to \texttt{EconLean}, a Lean~4 and Mathlib formalization of
graduate economic theory. Each result below is stated in ordinary mathematics, linked to the Lean declaration
that proves it, and given a proof that follows the Lean proof step by step, so that a reader can check the
argument on paper and then find the exact formal counterpart. The dependency graph shows how results build on
each other, from preferences and consumer theory through Berge's maximum theorem to dynamic programming and
macroeconomic models.
