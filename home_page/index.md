EconLean formalizes economic theory in Lean 4 with Mathlib: consumer theory,
general equilibrium, dynamic programming, growth, OLG, search and matching,
banking, and behavioral economics.

Every Lean theorem has a human-readable proof in the blueprint, written to
follow the Lean proof step by step.
