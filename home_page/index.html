EconLean

Economic theory formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib: consumer and producer theory, uncertainty, general equilibrium, dynamic programming, growth, OLG, search and matching, banking, behavioral economics and econometrics.

Every Lean theorem has a human-readable proof in the blueprint, written to follow the Lean proof step by step.
