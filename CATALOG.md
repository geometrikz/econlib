# EconLean catalog: every model and result we plan to formalize

Companion to [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md), which has the strategy, the foundations
backlog (F1–F26), the unifying layer (`Core`, bridges B0–B10), the two-step
verification protocol (§8.0) and the milestones (M0–M12). This file is the
model-by-model list. Compiled 2026-11-23 from the standard graduate texts,
handbook surveys, Nobel citations through 2025, and a check of what is
already in Mathlib or other Lean libraries (sources at the end).

**Columns.** *Key theorems*: what we actually prove. *Source*: the reference
the adversarial reviewer checks statements against. *Deps*: foundations from
PLAN.md §2 (F#), other catalog items, or `Mathlib`. *Diff*: E easy (days),
M medium (1–3 weeks), H hard (month+), R research-level. *MS*: milestone.
*Status*:
- 🟢 **ready**: everything it needs is in Mathlib or already in this repo.
- 🟡 **blocked**: waits on a named foundation item or earlier catalog item.
- 🔵 **import**: done in another Lean library (EconCSLib, Lyu–Li); port or depend.
- 🔬 **research**: not known to be formalizable with current tools.
- ✅ done.

Difficulty and status labels are my estimates, not measurements. Any entry
marked *(guess)* is especially uncertain. Every entry passes the two-step
check (PLAN.md §8.0) before it is marked ✅.
